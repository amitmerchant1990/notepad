Yahoo Notepad Is Being Discontinued. Here's a Simple Alternative.

If you’ve been using Yahoo Notepad to keep quick notes, lists, ideas, or bits of information, you may be looking for somewhere simple to move them.

Amit Merchant August 19, 2026 4 min read

Yahoo is discontinuing Yahoo Notepad, leaving longtime users looking for an alternative way to keep their everyday notes.

If all you want is a place to open a browser, write something down, and get on with your day, you don’t necessarily need a complicated productivity suite.

Notepad.js.org is built around exactly that idea: a simple notepad that works in your browser, without an account, advertising, or unnecessary clutter.

Yahoo Notepad is being retired

Yahoo has announced that Notepad will be discontinued on January 15, 2027. If you have notes stored there, make sure you download or otherwise preserve them before the service shuts down.

What is a good Yahoo Notepad alternative?

The answer depends on what you liked about Yahoo Notepad in the first place.

If you primarily used it for quick notes, lists, reminders, snippets of text, recipes, ideas, or things you wanted to remember, a lightweight web notepad can be a much better fit than a full productivity application.

Notepad.js.org is designed for this kind of use. Open it, start typing, and your notes stay in your browser.

Why use Notepad.js.org?

Notepad.js.org deliberately keeps things simple. There is no account to create and no setup process before you can start writing.

  • Free to use — no subscription required.
  • No account required — open the app and start writing.
  • Private — your notes can stay on your device.
  • Works offline — you can use it without an internet connection.
  • Installable — install it as a PWA and use it like a desktop app.
  • No ads — the writing experience stays uncluttered.
  • Customizations — a lot of customizations to make the app yours.
  • Ambient sounds — play background sounds to help you focus while you write.
  • Writing timers — set timers to track your writing sessions.

And bunch of other features that you can only experience once you start exploring the app.

A simpler way to take notes

One of the problems with modern note-taking apps is that they often try to do everything: databases, collaboration, project management, calendars, AI assistants, document editors, and more.

That’s useful when you need those things. But sometimes you just need a blank page.

That’s the philosophy behind Notepad.js.org. It is intentionally small and focused on writing.

What about privacy?

Notepad.js.org is designed with a local-first approach. Your notes can be stored locally in your browser rather than requiring you to create an account and upload everything to a server.

This also means you don’t need to hand over your email address just to keep a shopping list or jot down an idea.

As with any locally stored data, you should still keep backups of anything important.

Moving away from Yahoo Notepad

If you currently have notes in Yahoo Notepad, the first step should be to export or download your existing information using Yahoo’s official tools before the shutdown date.

Once you’ve preserved your existing notes, you can start using another note-taking application without having to recreate your entire workflow.

For people who mainly used Yahoo Notepad as a simple place to write and store text, Notepad.js.org can be a straightforward replacement.

Keep it simple

Losing a familiar tool doesn’t have to mean replacing it with something bigger and more complicated.

If what you liked about Yahoo Notepad was simply having a quiet place to write things down, that’s exactly what Notepad.js.org is trying to preserve.
